# LinkedIn launch draft

Android interview preparation should feel like learning—not jumping between dozens of disconnected tabs.

I’ve launched Android Study Studio, a free study workspace with 97 chapters across 41 engineering areas:

https://android-study-studio-satdev.web.app/

It brings together Kotlin, Android fundamentals, Jetpack Compose, coroutines and Flow, architecture, testing, Gradle, CI/CD, system design, and AI integration topics.

This version improves the study experience with:
• Continuous chapter reading, with explanations, code and practice together
• Topic search and progress tracking
• Adaptable 4-, 8- and 12-week preparation plans
• A responsive layout and dark mode

I also rebuilt the architecture around one shared reader and a generated catalog, so new topics can be added without maintaining another complete page layout.

If you’re preparing for an Android interview—or revisiting concepts for your day-to-day work—try a chapter and tell me what would make it more useful.

What topic deserves a deeper practical example next?
